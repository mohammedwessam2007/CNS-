# v15.2 Receipt: start from today and spread missed days where they are relevant

The owner's request (Wed 23 Sep 2026): *"Today is 23 September. Can we start from there and distribute the work of the 2 recent days on the couple of days where it's relevant, but keep doing everything I told you."*

Before v15.2, a learner opening the app on Wed 23 Sep with Mon 21 and Tue 22 undone got **FIRST-RUN CATCH-UP**: Day 1 (Mon 21) ran first, then Day 2, and today's lessons came after both.

## What v15.2 does

| Piece | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Start from today** | When the real date is ahead of the work day, the app moves to **today's course day** (Wed 23 Sep = Day 3). The real date and the original date of every moved lesson are both shown. Nothing is relabelled. |
| **Spread where relevant** | Each missed lesson is placed **just before the upcoming lesson it matters most for**. The measure is `lesson-relevance-v15.js`: a TF-IDF similarity over what the app teaches for each lesson (LEARN notes, professor lines, deep notes, facts), generated by `scripts/gen_lesson_relevance.js`. A lesson days away needs a stronger link, so foundations are not pushed far out. Part I and Part II of a topic travel together. |
| **Department order kept** | Within a subject, a lesson never lands after a later lesson of that subject. Anatomy stays spinal cord → brainstem → cerebellum → diencephalon → hemispheres. Histology stays neurons → PNS nerves → neuroglia. Physiology stays synapse → potentials → receptors → pathways. |
| **Balanced days** | The missed lessons plus the next *(missed days + 1)* teaching days are split into consecutive days with the smallest heaviest day. If a day would exceed 200 planned lesson minutes, one more day is added. Weekend review and mock days are untouched. If **today itself** is a review day and lessons are missing, today teaches them instead, and the banner says so. |
| **Progress kept** | A moved lesson keeps its original day's keys (steps, answers, pinned questions), so work already done stays done. A lesson you had started continues first. A spread-out day counts as done once all its lessons are done. |
| **Honest labels** | Chip: `TODAY · WED 23 SEP · +4 FROM MON 21 + TUE 22`. Banner **CAUGHT UP BY SPREADING · REAL DATE KEPT**: today's order and the minutes for each catch-up day. Each moved lesson shows `📅 From Mon 21 Sep · placed just before "Sensory receptors and afferents", the lesson it matters most for`. The course rail tags it `FROM MON 21 SEP`. |
| **Undo** | **Keep the original order instead** restores the v14 carryover behaviour exactly: every lesson back on its own day, progress kept. The carryover banner then offers **Start from today and spread the missed lessons instead**. The choice is saved at once and survives a reload. |
| **Safe moments only** | While a question is on screen (gate, options or answer), the spread waits for the next non-question step. It never yanks a question. At app start it applies immediately. |
| **Mocks** | After a spread, a mock asks only about lessons already scheduled on or before that day. |

If some of Day 1 was already done, the lesson you started comes first, and your finished steps stay finished.

## Not proven here

- **"Relevant" is measured by text similarity plus the department's own order**, not by a clinician. The Wed–Fri placements match standard neuroanatomy teaching (brainstem before cerebellum, synapse before receptors, PNS before nerve endings). Other weeks have not been reviewed by hand.
- **Catch-up days are heavier** (170–192 planned min against the usual 90–140). That is the cost of finishing two missed days within three.
- **Rolling back to v53** shows the course without the spread; the moved lessons reappear on their own days only in v14+ with the original-order choice. Returning to v15.2 re-applies the stored plan.
