# The Great Books Hour: rating, before and after (v18.13)

The owner asked (17 Oct 2026) for an honest rating of the self-education reading protocol, and then for everything needed to bring it to 10/10. This is that rating. It scores the protocol (what you read, in what order, and what you do before, during and after each sitting), not the books themselves.

## Rubric (10 criteria, 1 point each)

| # | Criterion | What a full point needs |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The canon | Works that change how one lives, across philosophy, novel, drama and short fiction, with no padding |
| 2 | Translation quality | Readable modern English, close to the original, in every sitting of the essential route |
| 3 | Dose and order | A daily dose that fits one hour, long and short works alternating, a finishable route (months, not years) |
| 4 | Preparation | A guide before each book: who is who, what to watch for, questions to carry |
| 5 | Active reading | Writing during or after every sitting, not just "I read it" |
| 6 | Memory | What you keep comes back for recall on a spaced schedule |
| 7 | Application | Every sitting ends in one concrete thing to do that day, and the next day asks whether you did it |
| 8 | Synthesis | Books are set against each other ("in conversation with the others"), and each book ends with a written verdict |
| 9 | Review | A weekly review, and one place (the commonplace book) that gathers everything written |
| 10 | Fits a real life | Any hour, offline, synced, never inside a medicine lesson, never changing the course |

## What a 10 here does not claim

- The new translations were made for this app. They follow the originals closely but have not been checked by a professional translator.
- Sittings outside the essential route ("Read every chapter") still use the older public-domain translations.
- Camus stays a study in our own words; his books and modern translations are in copyright.
- This rates the design. Whether it changes a life depends on reading every day, and only the owner can do that.
