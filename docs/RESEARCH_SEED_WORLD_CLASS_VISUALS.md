# Research Seed: World-Class Medical Visual Learning

This is a starting map, not a substitute for Opus doing fresh research.

The goal is not to clone a school or publisher. The goal is to synthesize the strongest proven visual-teaching patterns into a private, source-disciplined, low-friction tutor for one Kasr Al Ainy student.

## 1. Harvard Medical School HMX
Official entry point:
- https://onlinelearning.hms.harvard.edu/hmx/

Why study it:
- highly visual and interactive explanations
- dynamic animations
- concise concept videos
- true-to-life scenarios
- active-learning interactions
- clinical application woven into basic science

Use:
- **design and pedagogy inspiration only** unless a specific asset is explicitly licensed for reuse
- do not scrape or republish proprietary course imagery

INTELLECTUALITY should steal the principle, not the pixels:
`concept → visual mechanism → interaction → application → assessment`.

## 2. University of Michigan Medical School SecondLook
Official entry point:
- https://secondlook.med.umich.edu/

Why it matters:
- Neuroanatomy SecondLook uses images captured from University of Michigan plastinated anatomy and histology collections
- structured image-based self-review
- strong precedent for specimen-first neuroanatomy recognition

Use:
- permission-aware linking / referencing / educational use according to current terms
- verify each asset's reuse status before local caching or redistribution

## 3. University of Michigan Histology
Official entry point:
- https://histology.medicine.umich.edu/

Why it matters:
- real virtual microscopy and histology teaching
- strong tissue discrimination
- explicit educational licensing information

Current licensing note at handoff:
The site states its material is available under **CC BY-SA-NC 4.0** for noncommercial use, with restrictions around commercial use and commercial AI training. Opus must re-check current terms before copying assets.

Best INTELLECTUALITY pattern:
`real micrograph → low-power architecture → high-power discriminator → nearest look-alike → unlabeled recognition`.

## 4. University of Michigan BlueLink Anatomy
Official / institutional references:
- https://sites.google.com/a/umich.edu/bluelink/
- https://sites.google.com/a/umich.edu/bluelink/about-us

Why it matters:
- cadaveric photographs
- lab manuals
- interactive click-to-reveal testing
- 2D/3D anatomy teaching
- designed by University of Michigan anatomical-sciences educators

Best INTELLECTUALITY pattern:
`diagram orientation → real cadaver/specimen → click-to-reveal / unlabeled spotter`.

Verify exact asset permissions before embedding or redistributing.

## 5. UBC Functional Neuroanatomy
Official entry point:
- https://www.neuroanatomy.ca/

Why it matters:
- neuroanatomy videos
- cross-sections
- interactive modules
- 3D reconstructions / models
- MRIs
- stroke model
- regional brain images
- micrographs
- clinical neurological examination resources

This is particularly aligned with INTELLECTUALITY because CNS is the current course.

Best pattern:
`gross structure → cross-section → 3D orientation → pathway → lesion → clinical localization`.

Re-check reuse / embedding terms at the asset level before localizing content.

## 6. University of Leeds Histology Guide
Official entry point:
- https://histology.leeds.ac.uk/

Why it matters:
- virtual-microscope experience
- label on/off behavior
- zoom into regions of interest
- self-testing after visual exploration

Best pattern:
`scan → zoom → identify → toggle labels → test`.

## 7. Stanford Medicine 25
Official entry point:
- https://stanfordmedicine25.stanford.edu/

Why it matters:
- real bedside examination footage
- visible technique, not just text
- neurological examination / gait / reflex videos useful for clinical bridges

Use mainly for:
- clinical consequence cards
- neurologic-exam transfer
- practical visual context

Do not let bedside content bloat a first-year basic-science session.

## 9. Wikimedia Commons
Use only with:
- per-file license metadata
- medical relevance scoring
- human-vs-animal filtering
- image-quality threshold
- recent-use penalty
- provenance record

Keyword search is not enough. A medically irrelevant but keyword-matching image is a failure.

## 10. Research expansion mandate for Opus
Opus must perform fresh web research for 2026 resources before finalizing v14.
Search at least these categories:
- open neuroanatomy atlases
- medical-school cadaver collections
- neurohistology / histology virtual microscopes
- open medical animations / GIF-like mechanism assets
- neuroimaging atlases
- pathway visualizations
- clinical localization teaching

For every donor, record:
- institution / author
- URL
- exact asset / collection
- license or terms

- topic coverage
- visual modality
- why it is better than the current asset

Do not use "prestigious institution" as a proxy for visual quality. Benchmark actual teaching value.
