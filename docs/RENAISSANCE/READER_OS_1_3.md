# RENAISSANCE READER OS 1.3 · Reading Replacement Doctrine

Date: 2026-10-01
Branch: `renaissance/standalone-v1`

## Mission

Replace avoidable linear reading time without amputating knowledge, evidence, source access, primary experience, retrieval, transfer, or intellectual independence.

Reader OS is not a summarizer. Its unit of success is not "source compressed." Its unit of success is: **the learner can reconstruct, discriminate, retrieve and use the source's important content later, while the exact source remains recoverable.**

## Replacement boundary

Reading is treated differently by purpose.

1. Information-bearing nonfiction/textbook material may become a **REPLACEMENT CANDIDATE** only when source-retention, extractive-claim, structural, concept-coverage and retrieval gates pass.
2. Research can become a candidate only with the same gates plus a bounded methods/numeric-evidence audit. Exact consequential claims still point to source anchors.
3. Literature/poetry/primary philosophical or artistic text is **BRIDGE, DO NOT REPLACE**. Reader OS may replace orientation, secondary commentary and memory work, but preserves irreducible primary passages because language/form/voice can be the object.
4. High-stakes medical/legal/financial/actionable material remains **COMPRESS + EXACT CHECK**. Compression never becomes authority.
5. Failed gates produce **READ / RECOMPILE**, not a flattering summary.

## Source sovereignty

- Browser-local processing.
- IndexedDB library.
- SHA-256 source identity.
- Original source binary preserved when storage permits.
- Extracted/normalized source also retained.
- Full-source escape hatch always available.
- Exportable hash-verified Reader backups.
- Duplicate content deduped by source hash.
- Explicit delete only.
- No network upload path in Reader OS.
- Searchable PDF via pinned local PDF.js.
- EPUB/DOCX via local ZIP/XML parsing.
- ZIP bomb / expansion / entry-count guards.
- Scanned/image-only PDF fails loudly rather than pretending extraction succeeded.
- Unsupported formats fail loudly rather than silent mangling.

## Compression

The current compiler is deliberately extractive:
- structural source map;
- diverse high-salience exact-source sentences;
- top concept vocabulary;
- contrast/limitation anchors;
- methods/numeric/verification anchors;
- irreducible passages;
- cross-source lexical connection candidates, explicitly not claims of agreement.

Generated claims are forbidden in the baseline compiler. This is a safety choice until a grounded generative layer has a sufficiently strong entailment/evidence harness.

## Retrieval and proof

Cloze recall alone cannot prove reading replacement.

Reader OS 1.3 includes deeper prompts:
- argument reconstruction;
- evidence/method reconstruction;
- limitation/counter-position reconstruction;
- synthesis across major source claims;
- transfer to a new case with explicit self-audit.

Evidence lifecycle:
- NOT PROVEN
- ACTIVE RETRIEVAL
- PROVISIONAL
- DURABLE
- READING REPLACEMENT PROVEN
- for primary texts: SECONDARY LAYER POSSESSED, never "the work was replaced."

Proof requires performance over time. Uploading or reading a capsule earns nothing by itself. Deep-prompt performance participates in the thresholds.

## Time model

Linear-reading minutes are an estimate at 250 words/minute, never a fact.
Capsule minutes are an explicit rough model.
"Candidate minutes saved" are therefore a planning estimate, not an empirical claim.
Future receipts should compare estimated savings against observed user time.

## Anti-cheating / hostile requirements

The system must not:
- call a source mastered on import;
- call a source replaced from cloze-only performance;
- silently truncate oversized sources;
- silently accept scanned PDFs as text;
- silently accept unknown archives;
- treat lexical overlap as conceptual agreement;
- turn literature into a plot summary and call it possession;
- remove the original source after compilation;
- use a summary as authority for high-stakes action;
- allow source HTML/script content to execute;
- let ZIP containers expand without bounds;
- claim a generated sentence came from the source;
- confuse exact-source preservation with semantic completeness.

## Relationship to Renaissance curriculum

Reader OS is an additional organ. It does not modify the preregistered 52-item sealed Renaissance battery, the 32 authored baseline sessions, or medical CNS production.

The authored Renaissance curriculum teaches transferable primitives and cultural possession.
Reader OS metabolizes external reading into the same larger goal: more durable usable understanding per occupied life-minute.

## Current limits

World-class ambition does not erase current limits:
- scanned PDFs need a future OCR path;
- figures/tables are not yet semantically reconstructed;
- mathematical notation is preserved imperfectly by generic text extraction;
- generated explanation is intentionally absent from the baseline;
- deep transfer prompts currently use self-audit, not an independent semantic grader;
- "READING REPLACEMENT PROVEN" is an internal evidence state, not proof of equivalence to every benefit of reading the source.

These are frontiers, not reasons to weaken the truth contract.
