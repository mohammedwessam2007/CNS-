# Renaissance · 06 · Longitudinal simulation (Day 1 → Year 10)

A simulation, not a forecast: what the design says should happen, what would show it is not happening, and how the
screen stays the same. Every "expect" is a hypothesis to be checked against his own data (`05_BENCHMARKS.md`).

The screen at every horizon: **RENAISSANCE · TODAY · N MIN · one question · CONTINUE**, with the same six helpers.
What grows is behind it.

## How the UI stays simple while cognition grows

- The door's question gets harder, not the interface.
- Helpers never multiply: WHY THIS?, SHOW ME DIFFERENTLY, I ALREADY GET THIS, THIS DIDN'T CLICK, GO DEEPER, DONE.
- Growth appears only in receipts (counts with n) and in WHY THIS? (the record), never as a dashboard.
- As scaffolds decay, sessions get **shorter**: fewer representations, more unaided transfer, more reality.

## The honest caveat

Everything after Week 1 depends on things this build cannot guarantee: that he opens the door, that authored
seasons keep coming, that the delayed data are collected. The instruments exist; the results do not yet.
