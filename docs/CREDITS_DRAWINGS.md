# Drawings in the course

- **OpenStax Anatomy & Physiology 2e** (J. Gordon Betts et al., OpenStax, Rice University), licensed
  **CC BY 4.0** — https://openstax.org/details/books/anatomy-and-physiology-2e.
  139 figures, chosen per DIG-206 unit and NEU-205 chapter, resized to 1200 px and converted to WebP
  (`source/public/fig/ostx/`, built by `tools/g206/build_ostx.py`). Each figure in the app shows its
  figure number, its own credit line where the book gives one, and "CC BY 4.0".
- **Gray's Anatomy (1918)** plates from Wikimedia Commons, public domain (the "classic plates" layer).
