# Work less, live more: the course philosophy (v22, 17 Nov 2026)

## The one idea

**Study is a fixed budget. Life is the default.** The app's job is to put the most exam marks into the minutes you choose, and to send you away when they are used. Every rule below serves that idea, and each one is enforced by a feature, not by willpower.

## The ten rules

| # | Rule | Why (evidence) | Enforced by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **You set the minutes; the exam fits them, not the reverse.** When time is short, cut scope openly, never sleep or rest days. | Long hours cost sleep, and sleep is when learning is stored; rest after learning helps memory (wakeful rest g ≈ 0.45). | ⏱ My day: daily minutes, stop time, day off. "✅ Done for today" blocks new study; ✂️ What to drop cuts the lowest-weight chapters, never adds minutes. |
| 2 | **Only proof counts as readiness.** Never a feeling, never the bank. | The 16 Nov forecast came from the bank ("very good"); the exam gave about half. | 🧪 Blind check: new questions, checked by a second model, answered with the AI off, shown as a range. The bank score is labelled "past-paper mocks" with the real gap beside it. |
| 3 | **Know what you know.** A confident wrong answer is the most dangerous kind and the most valuable to fix. | Certainty-based marking (used in UK medical schools); hypercorrection of high-confidence errors. | Confidence on every answer, scored +3 / −6; traps; the CBM score in the header and the weekly report. |
| 4 | **You think first, the AI second.** The AI does what costs time without teaching; you do what the exam asks. | Unguarded AI: −17% on the exam; a guardrailed tutor: no harm (PNAS 2025). | Attempt first, hint ladder, Hint-only on unanswered MCQs, AI off in mocks and blind checks. |
| 5 | **Every mistake is an asset, and has a type.** | Spaced retrieval beats restudy (Larsen 2009, 6 months); different errors need different fixes. | Wrong answers become memory cards, re-worded on a spaced schedule. Each error is typed (never learned / misread / look-alikes / reasoning) with its own fix. |
| 6 | **Marks per minute decide what comes next.** | Past papers show where the marks are; most of them sit in a minority of chapters. | Chapter weights and examiner style from the bank; ▶ Today starts with reviews, then the lesson; triage. |
| 7 | **Your lectures are the first source; the bank is second.** | 16 Nov asked lecture content the bank never had. | 📥 slides PDF / notes photo import; 🔍 "What the past papers never asked" turns lecture-only points into cards. |
| 8 | **Study the examiner too.** | Each department has habits: negative stems, clinical stems, repeated traps. | Every explanation carries the examiner's style for that chapter (% "except", % clinical); 🎙 Viva; 📝 essay grading the department's way. |
| 9 | **Zero decisions, a clear start, a clear stop.** | ADHD: starting is the hard part; implementation intentions d ≈ 0.65. | One ▶ Today button on every screen showing the minutes left; Plan today writes the if-then lines; the day ends by itself. |
| 10 | **Honesty over comfort, always.** | A comforting number cost a real exam. | Ranges, not grade words; "not in your notes"; three examiners with the spread; an honest weekly report from real stats only; the post-mortem after every exam. |

The two parts that wait on you (they cannot be built from here):
1. **Your slides**: rules 2 and 7 work best when blind checks and lecture-only points come from the real lectures.
2. **One real run with your key**: everything is tested against a fake OpenRouter only.

## Not built, on purpose

Fixed Pomodoro, voice tutor, more 3D, streak pressure, heavy gamification: weak evidence or few marks per hour. Every one of them would cost study time, which rule 1 forbids.
